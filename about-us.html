About Us

About Us

Masar Al Taqadom Trading EST is a Saudi-based company established in 1990 by Engineer Fawzy Eltaweel,

One of the pioneers in the field with over 30 years of expertise. As one of the oldest and most trusted companies in the Kingdom and the Gulf region, Masar Al Taqadom has built a strong reputation for reliability and excellence.

The company specializes in importing high-density polyethylene (HDPE) pipe fittings used in water projects through both heat fusion and electrofusion welding. In addition, Masar Al Taqadom supplies a wide range of essential components for infrastructure and landscaping projects, ensuring comprehensive solutions that meet the highest quality standards.

MASAR is one of the biggest companies in Saudi Arabia that specializes in water management systems. Known as the slogan EVK. MASAR was founded in 1990 and has more than 30 years of expertise in the plastic water systems for irrigation and drinking. MASAR specializes in importing high-density polyethylene Compression fittings used in water projects, whether by heat or electric welding. The company also imports the necessary pieces for the infrastructure and landscape. Our mission is to be a leader in the manufacturing and trading of high-quality polypropylene and polyethylene fittings products for water systems, including drinking water and irrigation systems, and fire-fighting equipment. We are committed to providing reliable solutions that meet the evolving needs of our customers while prioritizing safety, quality, and sustainability.

Our Mission

At Masar Al Taqadom Trading EST, our mission is to deliver high-quality polyethylene and polypropylene piping systems and fittings for water, irrigation, and fire-fighting applications. We are committed to:

  • Supplying reliable and certified products that meet international standards
  • Supporting infrastructure and landscaping projects with complete solutions
  • Building long-term relationships based on trust and performance
  • Prioritizing safety, sustainability, and continuous improvement
  • Meeting the evolving needs of the water management sector in the Kingdom

Our Vision

To be the leading provider of advanced water management and piping solutions in Saudi Arabia and the Gulf region, recognized for innovation, quality, and long-term partnerships that support sustainable infrastructure development.
